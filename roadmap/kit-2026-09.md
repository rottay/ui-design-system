## 0. What this file is, and what it is not

It **is** the checked-in form of what the owner approved on 2026-09-05: the finite list of decisions a tenant
(or a vertical, within its envelope) can make about visual identity, each with a tier, a closed domain, a
declared fan-out target and an absolute minimum of families; plus the vertical envelope policy that says what
a vertical may lock for its tenants and what it may never lock.

It is **not** a plan document, a lane or a status. It carries no work orders and no findings. Its consumers are
WO-CAT-02 (which turns it into `contracts/theme/decisions` + `contracts/theme/catalog`), WO-CAT-03 (which
enforces the envelope in a single admission) and WO-CON-03 (which needs the decision list for the v2 document).

The kit does not derive channels. Naming is Standard / Pro / Internal only.

**Scope law** (binds every `Fan-out target` and every `Minimum families` cell of this kit; it qualifies the
approved cells without editing them):

- Modern is the only admitted engine (D-15), so every fan-out target and every minimum in this kit is a
  target measured on Modern.
- Where an approved cell names another engine — `in every shipped engine` in row 22, `rustic` in rows 21 and
  22 — that is the historical measurement carried along with the approved text. It is not a target for
  Classic or Rustic, which stay frozen and receive no content, token, test or accessibility work.
- `both transports` in row 28 means every origin of the single C4 ingress (static, document, preview,
  draft), not two competing compilers.

**Minimum-families rule** (mechanical, applied to every row; no value is invented):

1. If the row's approved fan-out target states a target ratio in the 25-family denominator of the cascade
   matrix (a ratio **not** prefixed by `today`, which marks a measurement of the current state), the minimum
   is that ratio, written `N/25 (declared fan-out)`. Rows 1, 11, 15, 16, 17, 22 and 24.
2. Otherwise, if the row is a `broad-scope decision` — rule 1 of the reference text words the clause as
   "every decision of wide scope (palette, typography, shape, density, rhythm, states, motion) also carries
   an absolute minimum of families fixed in the catalog, for example `>= 20/25`" — then the absolute minimum
   **is fixed in the typed catalog and nowhere else**. `>= 20/25` is the reference text's example, never a
   binding floor, and this kit does not turn it into one and does not invent a per-row number. Those rows
   are written `catalog-fixed (WO-CAT-02, owner-approved); reference example >= 20/25`, followed by any
   further target the approved cell already carries. WO-CAT-02 fixes the binding value in
   `contracts/theme/catalog` under owner approval. Rows 2-10, 12-14, 20, 21 and 23.
3. Otherwise the minimum is the row's own declared fan-out, written `Declared fan-out`: the decision must
   move every family it declares to consume. Rule 1 of the reference text adds no separate absolute minimum
   for these rows. Rows 18, 19 and 25-29.

Both labels name the same thing, the fan-out the row declares; rule 1 rows can state it as a ratio because
their approved cell already does.

A decision enters the kit only with a finite domain, a producer, a consumer and a causal probe, and the
number 29 is an outcome of that admission test rather than a target: both statements are approved text.
**Kit governance rule** (this file's own rule, not approved text): a row leaves or enters this kit only by an

## 4. Vertical envelope — D-28 (b)

**Shape.** `VerticalEnvelope` declares, for the vertical's tenants: the **available capabilities** (which of the
29 decisions exist at all for this vertical), the **bounds** of each available decision (a subset of the closed
domain, or a narrower numeric range, never wider than the catalog domain), and the **defaults** (the vertical's
preset value for every decision).

**The `locked` key set** (stated here once, and nowhere else in this file): `locked` accepts exactly 31 keys —
the 29 decision ids of section 2, plus `signature.accent-bar` and `signature.texture`. The mechanism exists
for every one of those keys; the contract shape carries `locked` in every case. `SanctionedOverrides` and
`profiles.icon` have no envelope entry at all: the first is the sanctioned escape hatch and not a decision,
the second is conditional and stays outside the catalog until its channel exists. What D-28 (b) fixes is
which of the 31 keys a vertical locks **by default**, and which of them `admission/envelope` must **refuse**
to lock at all.

**Who decides tiers.** `PlanEntitlement` owns tiers. The vertical envelope decides what exists and what is
locked; within what is not locked, `PlanEntitlement` (carried on `ThemeIntent.entitlement`) decides which tier
the tenant may activate. An envelope may not grant a tier and may not substitute for an entitlement check
(D-02: the vertical defines what exists, the plan defines what the tenant can activate).

**Refusal law.** Locking an entry of the never-lockable list is refused by name at admission, in every origin
(static, document, preview, draft). A lock without a written functional reason is not a valid envelope entry.

## 5. Kit rules carried with the approval

These five rules were approved together with the table and bind WO-CAT-02 and the derivation lane.

1. **Every decision declares its fan-out** (families and channels) in the typed catalog, and a causal gate
   verifies it: moving the decision between two values must change at least N computed channels in at least M
   families. So that a declared fan-out cannot be shrunk to pass its own gate, every `broad-scope decision`
   also carries an absolute minimum of families **fixed in the catalog** (**for example** `>= 20/25`), and
   not only the families it declares it consumes.
2. **No component channel is authored in a vertical**: it is derived (`derivation/chrome/<family>`) or it is a
   `SanctionedOverride` with a closed name (D-03, D-11).
3. **Target channel population** (a recommendation, not a measurement): about 29 decisions → about 300 semantic
   channels → about 1,800 derived component channels, all with a producer; 0 read without a producer (today
   2,589), 0 aliases without a date (today 1,533), 0 emitted without a reader (today 69).
4. **Per-axis tenant-difference probe, excluding colour.** Given two tenant documents of the same vertical that
   differ in exactly one group of the kit, measure per family the percentage with a computed-style difference
   attributable to that axis: shape (`border-radius`), typography (`font-family`, `font-size`, `font-weight`),
   rhythm (`padding`, `gap`, `margin`), depth (`box-shadow`, `border-width`), states (the `*-hover`,
   `*-active`, `*-selected` channels computed under `:hover` and `[data-state]`), motion
   (`transition-duration`, `animation-duration`). Threshold per axis: at least 80 % of the families that
   declare they consume that axis. Mandatory negative test: two documents differing **only** in palette must
   give 0 % on the six non-chromatic axes, and two differing only in `states.emphasis` must give 0 % on shape
   and typography. A global PASS requires all six axes at threshold.
5. **Evnto and rottay receive identity by decisions**, not by inheriting the neutral theme: the neutral theme
   is emptied of chromatic opinion and becomes purely structural (D-14).
