# Rotate North Star

## Product Character

Rotate should feel like:

- a control plane
- sharp, confident, operational
- dark, but not muddy
- dense, but not cramped
- premium through hierarchy and discipline, not ornament

## Desired Visual Traits

- large, calm primary headline zones
- one dominant action story per screen
- clearer difference between primary, secondary, and utility surfaces
- stronger contrast ladder
- fewer decorative overlays
- typography that earns importance through size, weight, and spacing

## Desired UX Traits

- operators can scan the page in one pass
- primary actions are obvious without hunting
- tables prioritize scan and triage over decorative chrome
- filters feel like fast operational presets, not a pile of controls
- edit modes and advanced utilities appear when needed, not all the time

## System Traits

- the shell is a product system, not app glue
- vertical identity comes from a coherent DS-owned grammar
- DB tenant customization is bounded, safe, and visibly effective
- every visible promise in the contract has a real reader
