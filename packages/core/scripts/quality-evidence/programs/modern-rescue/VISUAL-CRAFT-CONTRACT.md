# Visual craft contract — integrated, subtle, adaptive, configurable

This contract exists because mechanical correctness and token coverage can
still produce an ordinary or visibly broken product. It is binding for R1–R6
and is machine mirrored in `visual-craft-contract.json`.

## The standard

Modern must not resemble a default browser form, a 2000s administrative JSP,
or a collection of controls drawn independently in a paint program. Premium
does not mean adding more borders, pills, shadows, gradients or blue. It means
that anatomy, typography, edge, material, spacing, state, motion, iconography
and responsive composition form one restrained and intentional system.

Twelve reproduced reference incidents are now explicit blockers:

1. Steps connectors crossing labels and leaving an unbalanced empty region.
2. A tiny isolated Beta pill with generic typography and padding.
3. Transfer panels with accidental heights, detached searches/actions and dead
   space.
4. A desktop Select falling through to unrelated native-browser presentation.
5. Tiny Switches whose error/disabled states are little more than abrupt paint.
6. A compound filter with a rounded border inside another rounded border and
   no clear owner for either boundary.
7. One generic icon reused across unrelated objects/actions, plus unicode
   stand-ins and identical wells that erase semantic and tenant personality.
8. Charts presented as raw rectangles, arcs or cells with weak plot allocation,
   hierarchy, labels, interaction and data-state grammar.
9. Cards/detail panels that are only an outline around loose text, with no
   purposeful header, grouping, action posture or material/rule hierarchy.
10. Tiny pills used as headings, explanations and universal actions instead of
    a bounded compact semantic role.
11. Page headers built from competing full-width strips, uppercase copy, chips
    and rails rather than one information/action hierarchy.
12. Shell or workspace regions collapsing into character-wide columns while
    unused canvas remains available.

These are examples, not the whole checklist. The JSON contract closes the
general failure classes so a different component cannot repeat the same defect
under another selector.

The closed taxonomy contains twenty categories and 120 checks spanning anatomy,
typography, rhythm, boundaries, color, material, fields, choices, overlays,
data, charts, navigation, responsive, mobile, lifecycle, motion, icons/AI,
accessibility, i18n and CSS/theming governance. Every family reports every row
as `PASS`, `FAIL` or `NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_REASON`. Any applicable failure keeps
the family partial; an omitted category or fictitious N/A is a blocker.

## Integration rather than decoration

- One semantic region has one dominant boundary. A nested full frame is legal
  only for a separately named region with distinct interaction or hierarchy.
- Connectors, rules, motifs and decoration stop before content-safe zones.
- Sibling panels align optically unless priority deliberately differs.
- Focus, error, selected and hover treatments compose; they do not stack four
  unrelated outlines.
- Default browser chrome is never an accidental desktop result. A platform
  native mobile picker may be an explicit, accessible posture.
- Empty space has a reason. Responsive solvers must redistribute or disclose,
  not leave abandoned tracks.
- Typography has deliberate roles and optical padding. Compact does not mean
  miniature or illegible.
- A borderless surface is legal, but it still needs unmistakable grouping
  through typography, spacing, material or a ruled relationship. Conversely,
  an outline, radius or shadow alone is not a finished component.
- A chart is not accepted for drawing valid geometry. It must allocate its plot
  deliberately and integrate title, axes, labels, legend, tooltip/focus,
  annotations and lifecycle states without color-only encoding.
- Pills and icon wells are bounded anatomy, never a universal shortcut for
  hierarchy or personality.
- Motion explains relation, transition or state and disappears correctly under
  reduced motion.

## Optional icon and assistive intelligence

Components may expose governed leading/trailing icon composition. They must
also look complete with no icon: the slot and gap collapse fully. AI/sparkle
signifiers are permitted only when content is genuinely suggested, generated,
automated or confidence-bearing. They are never sprayed over ordinary actions
to simulate innovation. Icon posture remains profile-driven and all assets come
from the DS semantic supplier.

The current icon situation is a named migration defect, not acceptable visual
evidence. The reference torture probe reuses the historical compatibility
`TagIcon` dozens of times for unrelated metrics/actions and includes unicode or
span stand-ins even though the generated semantic corpus contains hundreds of
role-specific icons. R0 must drain the lab before R1. R2–R4 drain each family;
R5/R6 close the entire Modern-reachable UI and active reference fixtures.

Use the supplier-independent semantic `Icon` role facade or a focused generated
semantic pack. A functional glyph may not be a local SVG, emoji, unicode
character, legacy named-catalog placeholder or external provider. Explicit
exceptions are limited to chart/data geometry, QR/barcode/progress/rating
geometry, governed brand/provider marks and user media. Icon wells are
role/profile decisions, not a universal colored square around everything.
The chart/data exception covers encoded marks, axes and structural drawing;
toolbar actions, legend controls, statuses, annotations and navigation glyphs
inside a chart remain functional icons and use the semantic facade.

“Intelligent” layout means the component reacts to its actual container,
content, type scale, density and available actions. It uses intrinsic tracks,
priority, disclosure and mobile recomposition to preserve the task without
overlap, clipping, character wrapping or dead cavities.

## Palette replacement

The current BitHire and The Management colors are inputs to replace, not an
approved destination. BitHire must become a polished professional-network
workspace: cool near-neutral canvas, clean work surfaces, deep readable ink and
one disciplined professional-blue action hierarchy. The Management must become
a desirable monochrome editorial ledger: paper/near-white grounds, near-black
ink, grayscale hierarchy, ruled separation and accessible semantic exceptions.
Both must be attractive independently; The Management is not a deliberately
degraded or merely desaturated BitHire.

The palette travels through the existing static BrandTheme / DB TenantTheme
model. Families never branch on tenant and never hardcode the new identity.

## Human checkpoints

Claude may not propagate a new grammar inventory-wide. For each new layer or
shared grammar it implements three representative families, submits complete
paired evidence, and waits for Codex. After calibration, every cohort contains
at most twenty-five families and stops again. Changing the shared grammar after a
GO invalidates dependent receipts and restarts that cohort.

R1 has three four-canary checkpoints. R2 separates foundational/display/layout,
inputs, navigation, and feedback/overlay. R3 separates data, forms/non-chart,
workflow/navigation, customization/shell and charts. R4 separates structures,
page-surface groups, compositions and commercial. The exact closed cohorts and
artifacts are in `visual-craft-contract.json#checkpointPolicy`.
