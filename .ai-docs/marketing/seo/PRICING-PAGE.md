## Pricing Philosophy

### Principles
1. **Transparent** - No hidden fees, no surprises
2. **Predictable** - No per-MAU traps, no per-seat scaling
3. **Value-aligned** - Price on value delivered, not usage punished
4. **Simple** - Understand in 30 seconds

### What We're NOT
- Not per-MAU (Auth0 trap)
- Not per-seat (LaunchDarkly trap)
- Not per-event (Statsig trap)
- Not audit-fees-extra (Vanta trap)

## Pricing FAQs

### General

**Q: What's included in all plans?**
A: All 8 platform modules with 500+ use cases: authentication (80+), compliance (138), multi-tenancy (50+), permissions (40+), feature flags (30+), notifications (25+), navigation (74), and identity (98). No modules are locked behind higher tiers.

**Q: Do you charge per user/MAU?**
A: No. We don't penalize you for growing. Your price is predictable regardless of how many users you have.

**Q: Do you charge per seat?**
A: No. Add as many team members as you need without per-seat fees.

**Q: Are there any hidden fees?**
A: No. Unlike Vanta (audit fees extra) or Twilio (carrier fees), our pricing is all-inclusive.

**Q: Can I change plans later?**
A: Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect immediately.

### Technical

**Q: What compliance frameworks are included?**
A: All 15: GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI-DSS, KYC, AML, CCPA, BIPA, MiCA, Travel Rule, FCRA, AI Hiring, Responsible Gaming, PSD2, DORA.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes, [X days] free trial on all plans. No credit card required.

**Q: What databases do you support?**
A: PostgreSQL. We handle the infrastructure; you own your data.

**Q: Can I self-host?**
A: Enterprise plans include self-hosting options. Contact sales.

### Comparison

**Q: How does this compare to Auth0 + Vanta + LaunchDarkly?**
A: Those three alone cost ~$22,000/year at moderate scale, plus 8-12 weeks of integration. We include all that functionality and more for less.

**Q: What about Clerk? They have a free tier.**
A: Clerk's free tier ends at 10K MAU, then $0.02/MAU + $0.02/org. At 50K users with 100 orgs, that's $2K/month for auth alone. We include auth plus 7 other modules.

**Q: Why not just build it ourselves?**
A: You could. It took us 3 years and 500,000 lines of code. Calculate your engineering cost for that.
