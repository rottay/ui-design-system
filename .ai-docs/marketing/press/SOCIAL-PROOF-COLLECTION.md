# Social Proof Collection

System for collecting and organizing testimonials, reviews, and social proof.

## Collection Sources

### Direct Requests
- Post-onboarding survey (Day 7, Day 30)
- Quarterly check-in calls
- After successful implementation
- After passing compliance audit (SOC 2, HIPAA, etc.)
- After successful migration from competitor
- After first production deployment
- After renewal / expansion

### Passive Collection
- Twitter/X mentions
- LinkedIn mentions
- G2 reviews
- Product Hunt comments
- Hacker News threads
- Reddit mentions (r/SaaS, r/startups, r/webdev, etc.)
- GitHub discussions
- Discord community messages
- Blog posts / articles mentioning Rottay

## Approval Process

1. **Collect raw quote** - From survey, call, email, or social
2. **Edit for clarity** - Remove filler words, tighten phrasing
3. **Get approval on edits** - Send back for confirmation
4. **Confirm attribution** - Name, title, company, logo OK?
5. **Get written approval** - Email confirmation is sufficient
6. **Store in database** - Add to testimonial tracking
7. **Tag for use cases** - Mark relevant categories

### Approval Email Template
Subject: Quick approval on your testimonial

Hi [Name],

Thanks so much for the testimonial! Here's how we'd like to use it:

Quote: "[Edited quote]"
Attribution: [Name], [Title] at [Company]
Logo: [Yes/No]
Photo: [Yes/No]

Does this look good? Any changes you'd like?

Thanks!
[Your name]

## Usage Guidelines

### Where to Use
| Location | Best Format | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Homepage | Short quote + logo | "Replaced 6 vendors with one SDK" - CTO, Company |
| Product pages | Medium quote + context | Quote explaining specific use case |
| Comparison pages | Migration quotes | "Switching from Auth0 took 2 days, not 2 months" |
| Case studies | Full story + data | Detailed write-up with metrics |
| Sales decks | Logo grid + key quote | Social proof slide |
| Ads | Short, punchy quotes | "Infrastructure solved." |

### Format Options
- **Text only** - Just the quote and attribution
- **Text + headshot** - More personal, higher trust
- **Text + logo** - Company recognition
- **Video testimonial** - Highest impact, hardest to get
- **Logo only** - "Trusted by" sections
- **Case study** - Full narrative with metrics

### Length Guidelines
- Homepage: 10-20 words
- Product page: 20-50 words
- Case study quote: 50-100 words
- Video testimonial: 30-90 seconds

## Review Platform Strategy

### G2
**When to request:**
- After 30 days of active use
- After positive support interaction
- After successful implementation
- Quarterly for long-term customers

**How to request:**
- Direct email with G2 link
- In-app prompt after success moment
- End of call: "Would you mind leaving a G2 review?"

**Response strategy:**
- Respond to all reviews within 48 hours
- Thank positive reviewers specifically
- Address concerns in negative reviews professionally
- Never argue; acknowledge and offer to help offline

### Product Hunt
**Launch strategy:**
- Notify existing customers before launch
- Ask for comments, not just upvotes
- Engage authentically in comments
- Share behind-the-scenes content

**Comment engagement:**
- Respond to every comment
- Answer questions thoroughly
- Thank supporters genuinely
- Share roadmap updates

### Capterra
**Profile optimization:**
- Complete all profile fields
- Add screenshots and videos
- Keep pricing updated
- Respond to all reviews

**Review collection:**
- Include Capterra in review request rotation
- Feature in email signatures
- Post-support survey link
