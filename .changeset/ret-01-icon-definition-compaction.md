The generated semantic role modules state less. Each of the 282 role
definitions under `graphics/icons/semantic/generated/roles` used to restate
its own export name as `componentName` and to spell out `autoMirror: false`.
The generator now omits `componentName` and writes `autoMirror` only when
it is `true` (12 roles); `createSemanticIcon` derives the development-only
`displayName` from the role `name` and defaults `autoMirror` to `false`.

Nothing a consumer imports or renders changes. Every role icon keeps its
export name, its `SemanticIconComponent` type, its `displayName` (the derived
name equals the name each definition used to restate, for all 282 roles), its
default role and tone, and its mirroring behaviour. No migration is needed and
the codemod kit has no entry for this change.

The rows below exist because the contract gate fingerprints the text of a
published declaration, and the initializer of each role constant is part of
that text. They cover the 276 role icons the four pack doors publish by name:
`./icons/foundation` 139, `./icons/identity` 46, `./icons/intelligence` 46, `./icons/operations` 45.
The other six are the BitHire roles, which no pack door publishes by name; the
`./icons/bithire` door that did is removed, and `ret-01-icons-doors`
declares that removal. The `./icons/roles/*` export pattern resolves the same
generated role modules, and the same statement holds for them.
