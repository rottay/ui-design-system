Dark mode of the default (unauthored) theme is repaired: the foundation's
dark block now carries the inverted primary ramp, the primary role and its
paired inks, the popover dark legs, and the list/select/tree role reads —
an unauthored tenant in dark no longer paints near-black ink on the dark
ground, white-on-white list rows, or light panels. The Modern Calendar's
selected cell and active toggle read the new `--ds-calendar-selected-color`
channel (light paint unchanged: white). Frozen Classic/Rustic files are
untouched; their dark paint moves only through the foundation channels, and
every move was measured (the mount contrast table: zero pass-to-fail cells;
the frozen-carousel ink move is recorded in the lot's frozen-paint ledger
entry).
