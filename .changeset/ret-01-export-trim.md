Each removal is mirrored in `releaseSync.sourceEntrypoints`, the governed entrypoint manifest and its ceilings anchor, the consumer-contract disposition table with its code mirror, and the retired entrypoint wrappers under `src/entrypoints/` (78 of 79; `./styles` and `./styles/default` publish built CSS, not JS modules). The published map goes 122 -> 43 subpaths; the governed manifest retains only the CI-bound `./patterns/charts`.
