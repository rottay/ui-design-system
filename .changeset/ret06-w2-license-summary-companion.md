The graphics license-summary unit test adopts the WO-RET-06 W-2 (F-113)
retirement of the two vertical pictograms: its expected summary now counts the
six shared FeaturePictogram entries the governed manifest already lists, instead
of the eight it held before that retirement. The test was a missed companion of
that change; no runtime, capability, asset, licence record or public API
changes, and the pictogram retirement itself stays described by its own pending
major changeset.
