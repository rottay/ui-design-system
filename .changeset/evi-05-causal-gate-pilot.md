WO-EVI-05. The by-axis tenant-difference probe (`scripts/check/theme/axis-difference`)
is extended rather than forked for the pilot run: a family can be measured on its
own server-rendered anatomy, `focus-visible` joins the stamped states, the states
axis reads the non-chromatic longhands state channels paint (`transform`,
`opacity`, `outline-*`), running transitions are settled before a reading, and a
pair of complete tenant documents is cut one kit group at a time with both
negative controls of kit rule 4. The pilot population (the WO-FAM-01 families,
their declared axes and the catalog revision) is published by
`scripts/check/theme/population --pilot`.

No published surface moves: the instrument is internal and not among the
package's published `files`; the bump exists because `contract-changeset`
classifies everything under `packages/core/` outside `docs/` as shipped bytes.
