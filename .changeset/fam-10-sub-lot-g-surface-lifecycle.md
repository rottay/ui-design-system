WO-FAM-10 sub-lot G: `surface-lifecycle` (`structures/feedback/surface-lifecycle`,
skin `surface-states`).

ONE NEW CHROME DERIVER, `derivation/chrome/surface-lifecycle` (6
`--ds-surface-lifecycle-*` channels, resting at the exact fallbacks the skin
already read). Two rename the cut's only unproduced reads in its own
namespace: `--ds-stale-banner-padding-block/-inline` had no producer and no
family row that could claim one; they are the family's banner channels now.
Four carry the error boundary's governed paint: an 8% wash on the mode's own
canvas, a 30% edge on the mode's own border, the error ink and the elevated
action ground. Registration in `derivation/index.ts` is the DT's serialized
act for this sub-lot; the producer census reads deriver source, so the
channels are produced from this lot onward.

THE ERROR-BOUNDARY RULING, in the doctrine's terms: the boundary must
survive a stylesheet failure, so the inline part stays — but it is now the
MEASURED FAILURE-MODE FLOOR, never the paint. Twenty inline properties and
six colour literals drain to three properties on the root (contrast pair
plus a delineating edge), each riding a family channel with a literal
fallback; in a healthy tree the channels resolve and the literals never
render. The literals are per-mode (`light-dark`): a single light-only pair
was measured at 1.7–2.5:1 in dark scopes. The governed skin carries the
full paint — the frame padding, radii, the type scale, the description
rhythm and the retry button's strip — keyed on the stamped anatomy
(`data-part` `root/title/description/action` under
`ds-surface-lifecycle-error`). The retry control is a raw native button: a
browser default button is still a recognizable control in a broken tree, so
it needs no floor, and its paint is all skin.

TWO MEASURED REPAIRS over the inherited paint, both axe-proven in the
family's own causality gallery (empty debt pin across rottay, bithire and
evnto, light and dark). (1) Neither the boundary ink nor the banner ink may
read the system's status inks: `--ds-color-error-ink` was measured at
2.5–4.3:1 against this wash (the notifier, form-field and password-input
families share that token and its debt), and `--ds-color-info` is an accent
— the system's `--ds-color-info-ink` aliases it, and 2.3:1 on the banner
wash is a finding, not a strip. Both inks now mix the status tone toward the
MODE'S OWN text ink — the law section-frame's inks were adjudicated under.
(2) The banner wash no longer reads `--ds-color-info-bg`: that constant was
measured staying light in dark scopes (a light blue strip on a dark page);
the wash mixes the info tone toward transparent and rides whatever canvas
the banner lands on.

THE LOADING SURFACE is built by the shared anatomy renderer now: the
component stamps the family's canonical content anatomy (header line,
secondary line, rows) and the renderer draws one bone per measured part, so
the placeholder cannot drift from the vocabulary it stands in for. The
shimmer, the reduced-motion law and the forced-colours silhouette are the
renderer skin's; `surface-states` only sizes the source parts (the pinned
30%/20% header rungs and the row lines). The component keeps its public name
and its pinned landing classes (`ds-loading-skeleton__header-primary` and
kin) — the long-tail contract outside this lot references them. The loading
state announces once (`role=status` + `aria-busy` + label; the measured
source is aria-hidden).

DECLARED VISUAL CHANGES, each measured: the boundary border becomes the
alert arm's 30% translucent error edge where a solid pale red sat; the
description drops its 0.8 opacity for the full error ink; the banner ink
darkens to a readable info-text mix where the raw accent sat; the loading
rows are a uniform 16px rung where the retired primitive bars were. The
offline banner is unchanged — it was dropped from the family's axe gallery
because it carries none of this family's paint (it delegates to the Alert
primitive wholesale; the Alert warning arm's own contrast evidence is
Alert's acceptance to carry).

RESIDUE, routed not hidden: the `capability-anatomy` block this file still
paints belongs to `structures/feedback/capability-anatomy` (a separate
family, no skin of its own) — its 4 unproduced `--ds-capability-*` reads
and 5 consumed-not-stamped parts stay pinned until that family's cut claims
the block; no local producer is invented. The one skeleton-arm residue is
the public export name `SurfaceLoadingSkeleton` itself — a declaration the
gate's word-based arm counts — whose callers live in `surfaces/**` and
showroom outside this lot's write set; full zero wants a cross-lot rename
decision. The gate also reports `classVocabularies 1` where the pin was 0:
the family finally has a class vocabulary (`ds-surface-lifecycle-*`), and
one is the template's target. `--ds-loading-skeleton-header-height` retired
with the hand-made bars. No prop shape moved; every new channel is additive.
